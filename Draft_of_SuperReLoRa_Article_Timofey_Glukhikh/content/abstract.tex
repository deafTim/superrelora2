\begin{abstract}
% We will present an algorithm of training high-rank networks using low-rank updates.
Large language models are becoming increasingly larger, with the number of parameters in them growing correspondingly. Consequently, there is a need to develop methods to train these models while improving performance. We propose the SuperReLoRa method, which is based on the previously developed ReLoRA method. It utilizes low-rank updates to pre-trained models, allowing for a significant reduction in the resources required.
\end{abstract}
