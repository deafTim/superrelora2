\paragraph{Goal.}
We evaluate whether enforcing column-space orthogonality at merge-and-reinit, together with transition momentum that softens unfinished cycles, improves over standard LoRA and over ReLoRA when the training budget is matched.
Relative to ReLoRA, SuperReLoRa adds the orthogonal projection of $W_A$ at reinitialization~\eqref{eq:orth}; both multi-cycle methods use the same partial-merge momentum~\eqref{eq:partial_merge}--\eqref{eq:reinit_momentum}.
LoRA uses the same adapter parameterization but never merges.

\paragraph{Setup.}
All methods fine-tune \texttt{nicholasKluge/TeenyTinyLlama-160m} on WikiText-2~\cite{merity2016pointer} (train split for training, validation for evaluation).
Unless stated otherwise we use rank $r{=}8$, LoRA scaling $\alpha_{\mathrm{LoRA}}{=}16$, learning rate $2{\times}10^{-4}$, batch size $8$, sequence length $512$, and a budget of $8000$ optimizer steps (about $1.7$ epochs under our tokenization).
Adapters are attached to attention and MLP projections
(\texttt{q/k/v/o\_proj}, \texttt{gate/up/down\_proj}).
For ReLoRA and SuperReLoRa we merge every $q{=}500$ steps (16 merges in total), use transition momentum $\mu{=}0.2$, prune $99\%$ of low-magnitude Adam moments (then scale remaining moments by $\mu$), and apply a short jagged learning-rate warmup after each merge.
LoRA uses the same optimizer and schedule without merge-and-reinit ($\mu$ unused).
Experiments run on the Zhores HPC cluster (SLURM partition \texttt{gpu\_devel}, single GPU).

\paragraph{Methods.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{LoRA:} frozen base weights, single low-rank adapter, no merge.
    \item \textbf{ReLoRA:} periodic partial merge into $W$, random reinit of $W_A,W_B$ blended with momentum $\mu$, Adam prune+decay, jagged LR warmup.
    \item \textbf{SuperReLoRa:} same as ReLoRA, plus column-space orthogonalization of the fresh $W_A$ against the accumulated basis $U$ before the momentum blend.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Metrics.}
We report training loss, validation perplexity, and token-level next-token accuracy.
To probe the intended mechanism we also track (i)~the residual overlap $\|W_A^\top U\|_F$ immediately after reinit and (ii)~the dimension of the accumulated basis $U$ (or an effective-rank estimate of the summed update $\Delta W$) as a function of the number of cycles.
