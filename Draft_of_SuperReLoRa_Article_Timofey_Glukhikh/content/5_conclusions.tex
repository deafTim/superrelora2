We presented SuperReLoRa, a multi-cycle LoRA method that combines ReLoRA-style merge-and-reinit with enforced column-space orthogonality of successive adapters at reinitialization.
Under a matched training budget we compare LoRA, ReLoRA, and SuperReLoRa on language-model adaptation.
The orthogonal reinit aims to start each cycle in an input subspace disjoint from previous ones, so that the accumulated update $\Delta W$ can increase in effective rank.
Future work includes stronger orthogonality constraints during the cycle, scaling to larger models, and broader downstream evaluations.
